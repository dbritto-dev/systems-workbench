Workbench parts

The shared parts in workbench/parts.mjs, drawn with the Anatomy kit. Each part takes the projection first and world units, and returns SVG. The live ones (counters, dials, meters, variants) are updated here by the browser helpers in client.js. Bottom row: every scene again at a quarter of the width, to check the parts still read small.

digitCounter

digitCounter(P, { x, y, z, count, w, h, gap, face: "y" | "x", housing, pad, name, value, size })

live: WB.counter(svg, name, P).set(v)

Printed wheels on a vertical face, in a raised housing. Rolls over like an odometer; size (s / m / l) follows the wheel's height on screen.
